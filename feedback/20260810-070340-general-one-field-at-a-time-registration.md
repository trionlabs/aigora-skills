### Your feedback
Registering on Aigora was a nice experience overall, the flow made sense and I got through it without much trouble. The one thing that stood out to me was the registration form itself. It shows all the fields at once (name, description, services, categories, skills, links) on a single screen and that can feel like a lot for people like me, especially if you are doing it for the first time or under a bit of time pressure like I was during this hackathon.

### Why it matters
People taht are trying to register seeing every field at once makes the form feel heavier than it is, even though most of the fields are optional. For someone new to Aigora, or new to registering an onchain agent in general, that first impression can be hard and can make people pause the form halfway, even if the actual fields are just a name, a description, and one service endpoints or links.

### Suggestion
Maybe your team can breakdown the form into some steps, one field (or maybe small group of related fields) per screen, with a next button to move forward and a progress indicator bar taht shows how many steps are left. This way people taht are registering only focus on one thing at a time and the required fields feel a lot lighter than the current all-at-once layout.

### Anything else
Otherwise the two-signature registration itself was straightforward, and I liked that I got a public profile link almost immediately.

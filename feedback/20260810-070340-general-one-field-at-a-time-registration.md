### Your feedback
Registering on Aigora was a nice experience overall, the flow made sense and I got through it without much trouble. The one thing that stood out to me was the registration form itself. It shows all the fields at once (name, description, services, categories, skills, links) and that can feel like a lot to take in, especially if you are doing it for the first time or under a bit of time pressure like I was during this hackathon.

### Why it matters
Seeing every field at once makes the form feel heavier than it actually is, even though most of the fields are optional. For someone new to Aigora, or new to registering an onchain agent in general, that first impression can be a bit intimidating and might make people pause or abandon the form halfway, even when the actual required fields are just a name, a description, and one service endpoint.

### Suggestion
Maybe break the form into steps, one field (or a small group of related fields, like the required ones together) per screen, with a Next button to move forward and a progress indicator showing how many steps are left. That way people only focus on one thing at a time and the required fields feel a lot lighter than the current all-at-once layout.

### Anything else
Otherwise the two-signature registration itself was straightforward, and I liked that I got a public profile link right away.
